Soder-Spec

ABQMR has developed a low cost software defined radio spectrometer (Soder-Spec) capable of NMR spectroscopy and relaxometry. The Soder-Spec was designed to be affordable, portable, and easy to use. Data acquisition, analysis, and NMR operations are streamlined for users so that anyone can use it. ABQMR tailors the software and hardware for customers, so if you are interested in a Soder-Spec please contact us to discuss your needs and pricing.







Custom NMR Probes

Probes are a specialty of ABQMR. From surface coil probes for animal studies to in-ground root imaging probes, we have made them in all shapes and sizes for our customers.

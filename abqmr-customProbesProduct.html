Custom Probes

Probes Design for any Project

ABQMR designs and fabricates custom NMR probes for a variety of applications. We have experience making probes for super conducting magnets, permanent magnets, high temperature, high pressure, field use, human/animal studies, and more. We work with our customers to discover and address any and all needs they may have. Being a company that specializes in one-of-a-kind NMR hardware and systems, we welcome the challenge of designing and building something unique. We can design probes for existing systems or we can design complete NMR systems including the magnet, probe, and console.


Pricing

Each probe is custom designed and built to meet customer needs/specifications, so pricing is on a case-by-case basis. Please contact us to discuss your needs and pricing.
